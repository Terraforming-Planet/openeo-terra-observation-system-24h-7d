# Support WORLDIFACT × Terraforming Planet 🌍

**AI worlds made real — technology for observing, understanding and responsibly restoring Earth.**

We are developing **WORLDIFACT**, an experimental AI-assisted 3D creator, and **TerraObserver**, a research initiative combining openEO, Copernicus Earth-observation data and supervised AI for reproducible environmental analysis.

Our longer-term goals include:
- **Water and ecosystems:** study changes to rivers, wetlands, lakes, forests and glaciers.
- **Drylands and desert research:** investigate historical watercourses, soils, water availability and potential, carefully assessed restoration interventions.
- **Natural hazards:** improve observation and evidence-based risk assessment for floods, droughts, wildfires and severe weather. **No system can reliably predict the exact time and place of every catastrophe or earthquake.**
- **WORLDIFACT:** create 3D models, visualizations and simulations that could help people understand and explore environmental challenges.
